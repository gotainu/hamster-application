HamCare is a hamster care record and monitoring app. It organizes daily observations, weight, activity, temperature/humidity, and optional AI consultations. It does not provide veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

REVIEW ACCESS
Use the existing credentials entered in App Review Sign-In Information. The review account already has access to premium features through an existing no-charge subscription. No new registration, free-trial activation, payment, or payment card is required. Please sign in using email/password and open Today, Records (+), Changes, and Consultation. SwitchBot automatic measurements require compatible physical hardware; the app also supports manual records.

BUSINESS MODEL / iOS BUILD
This iOS build has no in-app purchase flow, purchase buttons, price display, external purchase links, promotional purchase calls to action, or Customer Portal links. Users who obtain a subscription independently on the HamCare website can use the associated features after signing in to the same HamCare account. Contract ownership and access are bound to Firebase UID, not an email match. General new Web purchases are currently paused; this does not prevent access by the existing review account.

The optional card-free trial starts only when the user chooses to start it, lasts 21 days, and allows up to 20 AI requests. Trial expiration alone does not create a subscription or charge. No trial reset or extension is used for review access.

ACCOUNT / DATA
Sign in with Apple, Google, or existing email/password is supported. AI and optional voice transcription have their own consent explanations. Account deletion is available in the app menu > App Settings > Delete account. The server confirms recurring billing has stopped before removing account data. Apple authorization revocation is handled as part of the Apple deletion flow, with manual instructions if automatic revocation is unavailable.
